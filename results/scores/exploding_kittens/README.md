# Exploding Kittens result profiles

These are the first standard per-rulebook cards generated by `generation/result_card.py`:

- `pdf_r2/` — original PDF agentic rerun;
- `clarified_r1/` — faithful TXT plus approved clarification appendix.

Both are historical implementations re-evaluated under post-hoc rubric `expl-v2.8`, with `n=1` and different generation protocols. They are not the prospectively frozen repeated main-study comparison. Future cards use at least three comparable runs and include structured material assumptions plus separate persona reviews.

No combined correctness score is reported. `result.json` is authoritative; `result.md` is the compact view.
