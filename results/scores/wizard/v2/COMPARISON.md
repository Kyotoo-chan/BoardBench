## Interpretation

The controlled clarification condition removed the clear Wizard-led legality failure and the deterministic-first-dealer deviation. This is evidence that the separately attributed clarification packet materially improved this implementation under the frozen evaluator. It does not establish a general model-versus-rulebook causal claim from one paired game.

One adjudication-dependent Jester → Wizard transition still fails after clarification. Thus clarification reduced, but did not eliminate, translation error. A repeated judge concern about persistent public played-card history remains a future regression candidate and is not added retroactively to the frozen comparison.

Only the successful declared successors are scored. Earlier pre-evaluation failures are not additional generations or independent replicates.
